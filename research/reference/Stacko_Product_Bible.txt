# Stacko — Product Bible
 
Oct 22, 2026 · @Nabih
 
## Overview
 
Stacko (ستاكو) is an AI-assisted stock system that shows every warehouse, item and shipment on one live map, so teams actually log their moves. Tagline: **Your stock, always in sight** / **مخزونك دايماً قدّام عينك**.
 
- **Product of:** Aiphin, Nabih's MENA AI agency (aiphin.me), shown as "A product by AIPHIN"
 
- **Who it****'****s for:** trading and distribution SMEs in Lebanon and the GCC with several warehouses and frequent imports
 
- **The idea in one line:** logging a move makes a truck drive on the map, so the visual is the reward for keeping data clean
 
- **First customer:** a client with 3 warehouses importing from China every month (below)
 
## The problem and the first client
 
The first client runs 3 warehouses, buys new and often unrelated products from China every month, and does not know his real stock. No one on the team logs transactions.
 
- He asked for a simple, AI-assisted system so the team can manage products and movements without heavy manual work
 
- He asked what it would cost, including AI credits
 
- Root cause: logging feels like extra work with no visible payoff, so it never happens
 
- Our answer: make logging take about 10 seconds (Telegram message, photo or quick form) and make every log visible on the live map
 
## Users and scale
 
Plan for about 10,000 SKUs and 20 staff across 3 warehouses, with new SKUs added every month.
 
| Role | Who | What they do in Stacko |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner / admin | Business owner | Watches the live map, stock, alerts and reports; approves adjustments |
| Warehouse manager | One per site | Receives shipments, approves transfers, runs counts |
| Staff | Floor team (most of the 20) | Logs in, out and transfers by Telegram, scan or quick form |
| Purchasing | Buyer for China orders | Uploads supplier invoices and packing lists, tracks sea and air shipments |
 
Every action is stored with who, what, when and where, so any stock number can be traced back.
 
## Features (phase 1)
 
Phase 1 delivers the live map, inventory, transfers with ETA, China shipments, Telegram logging and AI invoice reading, in Arabic and English.
 
| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Live stock map | Real map with each warehouse drawn as an isometric building, showing units and low-stock count |
| Warehouse panel | Items, quantities, capacity used, inbound and outbound, search across 10,000+ SKUs, low stock first |
| Transfers with ETA | Logging a transfer deducts stock at the source; a truck moves on the map with a live countdown; stock lands on arrival |
| China shipments | Sea (Shenzhen → Suez → Port of Beirut, about 30 days + 2 days customs) and air (Guangzhou → Beirut, about 14 h + customs), then a truck to the warehouse |
| Telegram bot | Staff log moves in plain text, e.g. "sent 40 LED strips from Beirut to Sidon"; AI parses and records it |
| AI invoice reading | Upload a supplier invoice or packing list (PDF, Excel or photo); AI creates the items and the incoming shipment |
| Alerts and questions | Low-stock and mismatch alerts; ask "how much of X do we have, and where?" |
| Roles and audit trail | Accounts for 20 staff; every move logged with user and time |
| Arabic and English | Full RTL Arabic interface with a one-tap language switch |
 
## Tech stack and data model
 
Stacko runs on Supabase (Postgres, realtime, auth, storage) with a Next.js web app, so every screen updates the moment someone logs a move.
 
- **Front end:** React / Next.js, Leaflet map with CARTO light tiles, SVG warehouse illustrations, Arabic RTL
 
- **Back end:** Supabase Postgres + Realtime + Auth + Storage; edge functions for AI parsing
 
- **Automation:** n8n for the Telegram bot and invoice pipeline
 
- **AI:** Claude or OpenAI models for message parsing, invoice extraction and stock questions
 
Core tables:
 
| Table | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| warehouses | Name, code, location, capacity |
| products | SKU, name (EN/AR), category, unit, supplier |
| stock_levels | Quantity per product per warehouse |
| transfers | From, to, mode (road, sea, air), legs, departure, ETA, status |
| transactions | Immutable ledger of every in, out, transfer and adjustment, with user and time |
| shipments | China orders with invoice files and line items |
| users | Staff, role, warehouse, Telegram ID |
 
Truck position needs no GPS in phase 1: progress = (now − departure) ÷ (ETA − departure), placed along the route's path. ETA is entered by hand or calculated per leg.
 
## AI usage and running costs
 
Expected running cost is about $80–150 a month, of which AI credits are about $20–50 for a team of 20.
 
| Cost line | Estimate per month |
| --- | --- |
| Supabase Pro | about $25 |
| Web hosting | about $20 |
| Storage, backups, monitoring | about $15–55 |
| AI credits (messages, invoices, questions) | about $20–50 |
| Telegram bot | free |
| **Total** | **about $80–150** |
 
AI credit assumption: about 100 Telegram messages a day plus a few dozen invoices a month on a mid-tier model, under $1 a day. To be confirmed against the client's real volume.

## Roadmap
 
Phase 1 ships the core system for the first client; phase 2 adds live tracking and more channels; phase 3 turns it into a product for other businesses.
 
- **Phase 1 — First client (6–8 weeks):** live map, inventory, transfers with manual ETA, China sea and air shipments, Telegram bot, AI invoice reading, roles and audit trail, Arabic and English
 
- **Phase 2 — Tracking and channels:** GPS on trucks for real positions, WhatsApp logging, barcode or QR scanning on mobile
 
- **Phase 3 — Product:** multi-tenant Stacko for other distributors, self-serve onboarding, pricing plans, forecasting and reorder suggestions
