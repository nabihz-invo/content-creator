# 1. Campaign Direction
 
Campaign theme: “Your stock, always in sight.”
 
Stacko is being developed for businesses that manage stock across multiple warehouses or branches. The campaign should make one idea memorable: a map-based view can help make stock locations easier to understand.
 
## November objectives
 
Help multi-location businesses recognize common stock-visibility challenges.
 
Introduce Stacko's map-based concept using honest, clearly labelled visuals.
 
Gather feedback from potential users before the product is fully developed.
 
Identify businesses interested in following the product's progress or discussing their needs.
 
## Content principles
 
One video = one pain point + one solution idea.
 
Use real prototype screens only for interactions that currently work.
 
Label concept animations as concepts; do not make unfinished features look live.
 
Use fictional demo data and avoid showing private customer or business information.
 
Use clear calls to action: comment, share a challenge, or follow the build journey.
 
Do not claim customer results, savings, or proven outcomes without evidence.
