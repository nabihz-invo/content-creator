# Stacko — product facts for content

## Overview

Stacko (ستاكو) is an AI-assisted stock system that shows every warehouse, item and shipment on one live map, so teams actually log their moves.

- Tagline: **Your stock, always in sight** / **مخزنك دايماً قدّام عينك**
- Product of **Aiphin**, shown as "A product by AIPHIN". Website: **www.stacko-lb.com**
- Who it's for: trading and distribution SMEs in Lebanon and the GCC with several warehouses and frequent imports
- The idea in one line: logging a move makes a truck drive on the map, so the visual is the reward for keeping data clean
- The problem: owners don't know their real stock; staff don't log moves because logging feels like extra work with no visible payoff
- The answer: logging takes about **10 seconds** (a message, a photo or a quick form) and every log shows up on the live map
- Scale it is built for: about 10,000 SKUs and 20 staff across several warehouses, with new SKUs every month

## Roles

| Role | What they do in Stacko |
|---|---|
| Owner / admin | Watches the live map, stock, alerts and reports; approves adjustments |
| Warehouse manager | Receives shipments, approves transfers, runs counts |
| Staff (floor team) | Logs in, out and transfers from the phone (message, scan or quick form) |
| Purchasing | Uploads supplier invoices and packing lists, tracks sea and air shipments |

Every action is stored with who, what, when and where, so any stock number can be traced back.

## Features (phase 1)

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Live stock map | Real map with each warehouse drawn as an isometric building, showing units and low-stock count |
| Warehouse panel | Items, quantities, capacity used, inbound and outbound, search across 10,000+ SKUs, low stock first |
| Transfers with ETA | Logging a transfer deducts stock at the source; a truck moves on the map with a live countdown; stock lands on arrival |
| Imports (China) | Sea (Shenzhen → Suez → Port of Beirut, about 30 days + about 2 days customs) and air (Guangzhou → Beirut, about 14 h + customs), then a truck to the warehouse |
| Logging from the phone | Staff log moves in plain text, e.g. "sent 40 LED strips from Beirut to Sidon"; AI parses and records it. (It runs on a Telegram bot, but posts say "from your phone" and never show the Telegram name or logo.) |
| AI invoice reading | Upload a supplier invoice or packing list (PDF, Excel or photo); AI creates the items and the incoming shipment |
| Alerts and questions | Low-stock and mismatch alerts; ask "how much of X do we have, and where?" |
| Roles and audit trail | Accounts for the team; every move logged with user and time |
| Arabic and English | Full RTL Arabic interface with a one-tap language switch |
| Online store connection (WooCommerce, live; owner 10 Nov 2026) | The shop appears in Stacko as one "Online store" location. Its products and stock update in Stacko on their own; moves into or out of that location change the shop's stock. Posts name WooCommerce in the caption only (no third-party logos on visuals). It does not reserve stock in a warehouse or track customer deliveries. |
