For importers, stock spends weeks "somewhere". Knowing which leg it's on changes how you sell it.

Factory to port, the sea (about a month from Shenzhen via Suez to Beirut) or the air (hours), customs (often a couple of days, the least predictable), then the truck to your warehouse.

In Stacko, an incoming shipment shows each leg with its ETA on the map, and the stock joins the warehouse only when it's received.

Durations are typical and vary by carrier and season.
