A customer asks for 450 units. Your records say 500. Can you say yes?

Only if 450 are available. 80 may already be promised to orders waiting to go out, and the container at sea doesn't count until it's received.

On hand is what you have. Available is what you can promise. Stacko shows on hand, committed, available and incoming for every item in every warehouse.
