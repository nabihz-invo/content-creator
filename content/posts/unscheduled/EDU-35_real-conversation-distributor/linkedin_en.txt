A distributor told me something last week that I haven't stopped thinking about.

I asked: "What's the hardest part of managing stock across your three warehouses?"

I expected him to say "the software" or "the reporting."

He said: "Honestly? It's the phone calls. Every morning, I call the three warehouse managers. Every morning, I hear three different versions of the truth."

Three different versions of the truth.

Not because anyone was lying. Because each manager sees their own warehouse, and nobody sees all three.

That's not a people problem. It's a visibility problem.

And it's the reason Stacko works the way it does — one map, all locations, one version of the truth.

If you manage stock across multiple locations, I'd love to hear: what's the hardest part for you?
