Title: No, our AI does not change your stock automatically

CAPTION
"Will your AI change my stock automatically?" No, and that's on purpose.

Stacko's AI reads the supplier invoice and prepares a draft. Drafts don't touch your stock. Anything new or unclear is flagged, never guessed. An authorised person approves, and only then is it recorded, with who, what and when.

AI should speed up the boring part, not take responsibility away from your team.

Questions about how it works? Ask below, or book a meeting with our team. Link in bio.
