Who can create a transaction, and who can approve it?

If the answer is "the same person," ask why. Separation of duties is how errors and unauthorized changes get caught.

Are permissions scoped by action and location?

A warehouse user in Beirut shouldn't be able to adjust stock in Tripoli. Granularity matters.

Is there an audit trail with before/after values?

"Who changed this, when, and from what to what?" If the system can't answer, it can't be trusted with stock.
