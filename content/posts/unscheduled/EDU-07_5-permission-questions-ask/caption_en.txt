Before you buy *any* inventory system — including ours — ask these 5 questions:

01 Who can create a transaction, and who can approve it?
02 Are permissions scoped by action *and* location?
03 Is there an audit trail with before/after values?
04 What happens when the AI gets something wrong?
05 What is the source of truth for each data type?

If a vendor can't answer all five clearly, that's your answer.
