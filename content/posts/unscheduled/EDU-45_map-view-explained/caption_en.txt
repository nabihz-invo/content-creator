The map view, explained.

Every warehouse. Every branch. One screen.

01 Locations, not spreadsheets — each warehouse and branch is a pin, not a row
02 Click a pin, see the stock — quantities, status, availability
03 See movement, not just quantities — what moved between locations
04 Spot what needs attention — low stock and pending items are visually distinct (Concept)
05 Scoped to permissions — warehouse users, managers, and viewers each see what they should

What the map is *not*: a replacement for your ERP ledger. It's the visual layer that answers "where is it?" — fast.
