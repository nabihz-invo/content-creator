The 6 objections we hear most. And our honest answers.

1️⃣ "We already have an ERP." → Good. Keep it. Stacko can layer on top or work as a full system.
2️⃣ "It's probably too expensive." → We haven't finalized pricing. We scope by location, users, AI volume. If it's not a fit, we'll say so.
3️⃣ "My team won't use it." → Role-scoped views mean each person sees exactly what they need.
4️⃣ "We're too small." → If you have one location and one person, you might not need us yet. Multiple locations? Probably.
5️⃣ "AI will make mistakes." → Yes. Every AI does. Ours creates drafts, flags uncertainty, and requires human approval.
6️⃣ "What if we want to switch later?" → Your data is yours. We define retention before you sign.

Have a different objection? Bring it to a meeting. We'd rather answer it than have you wonder.
