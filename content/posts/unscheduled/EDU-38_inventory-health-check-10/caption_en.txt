The inventory health check: 10 questions to ask yourself before your next stocktake.

01 Can you answer "where is my stock?" in under 10 seconds?
02 Do you know which transactions are pending approval right now?
03 Can you trace every movement to a user and a timestamp?
04 Are permissions scoped by action and location?
05 Is the creator of a transaction different from the approver?
06 Do you have duplicate protection on invoices and imports?
07 Can you see stock across all locations in one view?
08 Does your team know the source of truth for each data type?
09 Can you onboard a new employee without full access?
10 If a customer asks for 40 units right now, can you answer confidently?

How many did you answer "no" to? Every "no" is a gap.
