"Who changed this stock quantity?"

If that question makes you nervous, you don't have a permissions problem. You have a *trust* problem.

Stacko's role model separates responsibilities on purpose:

1️⃣ Invoice/document user — uploads, drafts
2️⃣ Purchase user — purchase drafts & receipts
3️⃣ Warehouse/stock user — receipts, issues, transfers, adjustments (assigned locations only)
4️⃣ Manager/admin reviewer — approves, resolves, manages permissions
5️⃣ Administrator — configures
6️⃣ Billing — subscription only
7️⃣ Read-only viewer — sees permitted data, edits nothing

The person who creates a transaction shouldn't be the only person who approves it. That's not bureaucracy — that's how you keep records reliable.
