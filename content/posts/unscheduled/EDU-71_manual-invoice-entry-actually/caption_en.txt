What is manual invoice entry actually costing you?

It's more than the typing time.

The visible cost: if someone spends 2 hours a day typing invoice data, that's 40 hours a month — a full work week, every month.

The hidden costs: typos that become wrong stock. Duplicate invoices paid twice. Discrepancies discovered weeks later. The opportunity cost of a smart employee doing data entry.

Run the math with your own numbers:
Hours per week × hourly cost × 52 = annual cost
Plus one duplicate invoice per month.
Plus one lost customer per quarter.

What AI changes: the typing becomes reviewing. Upload → extract → draft → review → approve.
What AI doesn't change: the need for accuracy, approval, and an audit trail.
