Title: Will AI make mistakes? Yes. Here's why that's fine.

CAPTION
"Will your AI make mistakes?" Yes. Every AI does. Anyone who says otherwise is selling, not building.

The real question is what happens when it does. In Stacko, the AI only prepares drafts, anything new or unclear is flagged, and stock changes only after someone with approval rights confirms. Every entry keeps who, what and when.

If an AI tool can't tell you what happens when it's wrong, keep looking.

Questions? Ask below, or book a meeting with our team. Link in bio.
