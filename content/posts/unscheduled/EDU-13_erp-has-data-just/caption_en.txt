Your ERP has the data. You just can't see it.

ERPs are built for records, not for sight. Yours knows every transaction — but "where is my stock across three warehouses?" usually means opening a report, filtering, exporting, and squinting at a table.

Stacko can sit on top of an existing system through an API or supported connector — displaying synchronized locations and stock on a visual map. Read-only visibility when you want it. Draft-for-approval when you need review. Approved write-back where supported.

Before any sync: we define the source of truth per data type. No duplicate records. No conflicting values. Sync status, errors, conflicts, and timestamps visible.

We're not asking you to abandon your ERP. We're adding the visual and AI layer it never had.
