Every stock manager asks three questions. Every day.

1. "Where is it?"
2. "What moved?"
3. "Who changed it?"

They sound simple. They're not.

"Where is it?" means: I have stock across multiple locations and no single view. I need to answer a customer in seconds, not phone calls.

"What moved?" means: something happened to my inventory and I need to trace it. Not guess. Trace.

"Who changed it?" means: I need to trust my records. Which means I need to know who created, who approved, and when — without digging through chat history.

Most inventory tools answer one of these well. The others are an afterthought.

We built Stacko around all three:

Visibility — the map.
Movement — the transaction history.
Control — the roles and audit trail.

If you're a stock manager, I'd love to know: which of the three questions do you ask most often?
