The end-of-year stocktake is coming.

6 steps to survive it:

01 Freeze the noise — pause non-essential movements during the count
02 Count in zones, not all at once — one zone, one owner, one count sheet
03 Record discrepancies immediately — not "later"
04 Separate the counter from the approver — separation of duties catches honest mistakes
05 Compare against the map, not a spreadsheet
06 Write down what you learned — the pattern matters more than the number
