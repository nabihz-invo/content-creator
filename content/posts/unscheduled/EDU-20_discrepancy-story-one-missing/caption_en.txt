One missing transfer. One lost customer.

Branch A had 20 units. Branch B needed 15. The transfer was agreed on WhatsApp. The stock moved. Nobody recorded it.

A week later, a customer at Branch B ordered 15 units. The records showed 5. The physical stock was gone. The customer left.

Then came the investigation. Stolen? Miscounted? Wrong branch? The WhatsApp thread was deleted. The spreadsheet was overwritten. The answer was gone.

This is what happens when stock movements aren't recorded. Not because anyone was careless — because the system didn't make recording easy.

Stacko records every transfer, receipt, issue, and adjustment — with the user, the timestamp, and the from/to locations. One transaction. One audit entry. One answer.

Records are not bureaucracy. They're how you protect your team, your customers, and your business.
