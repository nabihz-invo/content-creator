The 5-day stock visibility challenge.

5 days. 5 small steps. A clearer view by Friday.

Day 1 — Count your systems: how many places does your stock data live?
Day 2 — Ask your team: "What's the hardest part of your day?"
Day 3 — Trace one movement: did it get recorded? By whom? When?
Day 4 — Share what you found (anonymized if you prefer)
Day 5 — We'll post the most common patterns and what they mean

No product pitch. Just a week of seeing your own setup more clearly.
