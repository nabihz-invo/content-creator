CAPTION
"We already have Odoo. We already have SAP." Good. Keep it.

ERPs are excellent at recording transactions. Seeing where your stock is across every site is another story: a report, a filter, an export, a squint.

Stacko sits on top of your system and adds the live map and AI document capture. You choose the limits (read-only, draft for approval, or send after approval) and every sync carries a reference, so nothing is recorded twice.

Keep your ERP, add the view. Book a meeting to discuss your setup. Link in bio.
