Materials in. Finished goods out.

Can you see both at the same time?

Manufacturers run two inventories at once: raw materials and components on one side, finished goods on the other. Most systems track one well and treat the other as an afterthought.

The result: quiet stockouts. Finished goods you can't build the next batch for. Raw materials sitting unused while a finished-goods shortage blocks a shipment.

Stacko puts both on one map. Location-level stock for materials and finished goods in the same view. Receipts, issues, transfers, adjustments: every movement traced with user and timestamp. Each person records their own part, and the manager approves.

Stacko is the stock management system. Raw materials and finished goods on one map.
