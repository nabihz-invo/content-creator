How to evaluate inventory software in 30 minutes.

The framework we'd use if we were buying:

01 Visibility — can you see stock across every location in one view?
02 Movement tracing — can you trace every movement to a user and timestamp?
03 Control — are permissions scoped? Is the creator different from the approver? Is there an audit trail?
04 Automation — does AI create drafts or make decisions? Are uncertain fields flagged or guessed?
05 Fit — does it work alongside your ERP? Is the source of truth defined?

Score each category 1–5. Total out of 25. Anything below 18 is a warning. Below 12 is a no.

Use this on us first. Then use it on everyone else. May the best system win.
