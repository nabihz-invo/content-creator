Pain point: Warehouse locations can feel disconnected when viewed separately.

One solution to introduce: Stacko's vision for a more connected, map-based way to view stock locations.

Scene 1 | 0–4 sec

Visual: A map with several warehouse pins appearing one by one.

Voice-over: “What if your warehouse locations were easier to see at a glance?”

On-screen text: Meet Stacko.

Scene 2 | 4–11 sec

Visual: Show the map concept, followed by actual prototype screens that are ready to demonstrate.

Voice-over: “We're developing a stock-management tool built around a simple idea: your stock locations should be easier to understand.”

On-screen text: Stock visibility, reimagined.

Scene 3 | 11–21 sec

Visual: Zoom in on two warehouse pins and illustrate connected locations. Do not depict unbuilt controls or transactions as working features.

Voice-over: “Our goal is to give businesses managing multiple locations a clearer way to look at their stock operation.”

On-screen text: Built for businesses with multiple locations.

Scene 4 | 21–30 sec

Visual: Stacko logo.

Voice-over: “Stacko is still in development. Follow our journey and help shape what comes next.”
