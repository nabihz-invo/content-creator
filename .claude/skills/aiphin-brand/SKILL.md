# Aiphin brand guide

Aiphin is an AI and automation agency for the MENA region, based in Beirut and working across Lebanon, the Gulf and remote markets. It is a sister agency to Invocom with its own brand and its own mascot: **Aiphin, the AI dolphin**. The name is AI + dolphin, said **AY-fin**; in Arabic it is written **أيفِن**.

**Positioning:** *One team. Every expert.* Business consulting, customer support, operations automation, AI integrations, business diagnostics and CRM tools, all from one team that **builds what it recommends and stays to run it.**

Aiphin is a separate brand. Don't use Invocom's logo, red, navy or Monument Extended in Aiphin work, and don't mention Invocom in public-facing material unless the user asks for co-branding.

## 1. Services

**The core idea:** most agencies sell either the advice or the build. Aiphin does both, because a roadmap nobody implements and a system nobody planned fail the same way. Lead with the business problem, not the AI: *"Nobody wakes up wanting AI. They want the WhatsApp answered, the quote out before lunch, and the stock number to be right."*

### Pain points we solve (use for hooks, ads and openers)

- The same customer questions get answered by hand, fifty times a week, in three different places.
- Sales lives in spreadsheets, delivery lives in the ERP, and neither side trusts the other's numbers.
- Leads arrive at 9pm and get a reply at 11am the next day, by which time they've booked elsewhere.
- Reporting is someone's Thursday, every Thursday, rebuilt from scratch in Excel.

### How we work

**Diagnose** (sit with the work: shadow the people doing the task, count the steps, tools and waiting) → **Quantify** (put a monthly cost on each bottleneck, so numbers settle the priorities) → **Design** (pick the smallest fix: an integration, a bot, or deleting a step) → **Prove** (ship one real workflow live before anyone signs off on a bigger programme).

| When | Phase | Client receives |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1–2 | Business diagnostic: interviews, a map of current systems, a ranked list of what costs most | A written diagnostic, a process map and a costed opportunity list, theirs to keep whatever they decide |
| Week 3–6 | First workflow live: the highest-value fix, built end to end, tested with real data and used by the team | One production workflow, its documentation, and before-and-after numbers |
| Week 7–14 | Roll out the rest: remaining integrations, CRM or site, dashboards, content systems, in sequence | The wider platform, staff training and an adoption plan per team |
| Ongoing | Run and improve: monitoring, exceptions, retraining the assistants, a monthly review | Monthly reporting, agreed response times and a quarterly roadmap they sign off |

### Why companies pick us

1. **We diagnose before we quote.** A costed picture of the problem first. If automation isn't the answer, we say so.
2. **Strategy and build in one team.** The person who scoped it builds it; nothing gets lost in a handover.
3. **Live in weeks, not quarters.** One working process in production early, so clients judge output, not a Gantt chart.
4. **Everything is documented.** Diagrams, credentials and runbooks handed over as we go. If a client replaces us, they keep a working system.
5. **Built for how business runs here.** WhatsApp-first customers, Arabic and English, cash and cheque realities, and teams that won't adopt a slow tool.
6. **We stay after launch.** Automations are maintained on a retainer instead of quietly rotting.

### Lockups with the character

- **Stacked** (main logo): Aiphin above the wordmark, centered. The wordmark is about 1.1× the character's width; the gap is about half the cap height. Use it for posters, social posts, the website hero and end cards.
- **Horizontal**: the wordmark on the left, Aiphin on the right **looking toward the name** (he faces left in the master image). The cap height is about 30% of the character's height. Use it for headers, email signatures and deck title slides.
- **Bilingual**: AIPHIN on top, أيفِن below, both centered, with or without Aiphin above them. The Arabic block (alef top to the dots under ي) is 1.5× the English cap height, with a gap of about 0.4× the cap height. Use it for the website header, business cards, signage and decks read in both languages.
- **Arabic stacked**: Aiphin above أيفِن, with the Arabic about 0.95× the character's width. Use it for Arabic posters and social posts.
- **Arabic horizontal (right to left)**: Aiphin on the left, **mirrored so he faces right toward his name**, and أيفِن on the right. The Arabic block is about 46% of the character's height.
- **Wordmark only**: when the character won't fit or would be smaller than about 64 px tall.
- **Avatar / app icon**: Aiphin's head and shoulders in a circle or a rounded square on the Ocean gradient. Use it for the chat widget, WhatsApp and Instagram profile pictures and the app icon.

### Rules

- Clear space: at least the height of the letter "A" on every side.
- Minimum size: wordmark 80 px wide on screen (20 mm in print); avatar 32 px.
- Don't recolor outside the table above, stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows to or re-space the wordmark, and don't set it in another font.
- Never mirror either wordmark. The character may be mirrored (see section 4); the names never are.
- Never set أيفِن in another Arabic font, and always spell it أيفِن (إيفن reads "EE-fin").
- Glow (`#2EF2EE`) is for dark backgrounds only; on white it disappears, so use Signal there.

## 4. The character: Aiphin

**Look (keep every detail):** a stylized 3D dolphin with a polished, Pixar-like render, soft studio lighting and glossy skin.
- **Body and face:** light sky-blue body (`#79C0DE`) with a white-grey belly and chin, a short rounded snout, and big friendly blue eyes with lashes and white highlights.
- **Gear:**
  - a white wraparound AR visor with a clear lens and faint cyan HUD lines;
  - a white headset with a glowing cyan ear-light and a thin mic boom;
  - a glass cylinder backpack with a glowing cyan neural-circuit "brain" core and white end caps;
  - a white belt with a small glowing cyan chip buckle.
- **Glow:** cyan, only on the tech parts.
- **Shape:** curved dorsal fin, two flippers and a tail fluke. In the master image he floats in a three-quarter view, facing the viewer's left.

**Personality:** smart, calm, helpful and a little playful. He is the agency's AI guide: he explains, points the way and celebrates results. He is never goofy, sarcastic or scary.

**How to use him**
- He looks toward the text, product or CTA he is presenting. He faces left by default. If the layout needs him facing right (for example an Arabic layout with him on the left), mirror the image horizontally. That's fine for him; it's never fine for the wordmark.
- Keep him in his 3D style. Don't trace him into flat vector art, change his colors, remove the visor, headset, backpack or belt, or add other brands' logos to his gear.
- Give him room: about 25 to 40% of a social post, the full height in a stacked lockup, a circle crop for avatars.
- On dark backgrounds, add a soft cyan halo behind him (Glow at about 30%, large blur).
- The master image is 1024 px (the character is about 600 px tall). For banners and large print, ask for a 4K export from Gemini instead of upscaling.

**New poses and scenes (Gemini / Nano Banana prompt)**

## 6. Voice

Aiphin sounds like a senior consultant who also builds: **practical, plain-spoken, confident, helpful, a little playful through the mascot.** The website copy in section 1 is the reference for the tone. It is problem-first and concrete, it pushes back on hype, and it is honest ("If automation isn't the answer, we say so").

| Pillar | Means |
|---|---|
| Practical | Lead with the business pain (the unanswered WhatsApp, the Thursday Excel report), then the fix. AI is the means, never the headline promise. |
| Clear | Short sentences. One idea at a time. Explain AI in plain words, without jargon. |
| Confident | We know how to do this. No hedging, no "we try to". |
| Results-first | Talk about outcomes: hours saved, tickets answered, replies at 9pm instead of 11am the next day. Use the approved proof points or numbers the user gives, nothing else. |
| Helpful | Aiphin guides; he doesn't hype. Every piece ends with a clear next step. |
| Playful (light touch) | A wink from the dolphin is fine ("Let's dive in", "Making waves"). Use at most one sea pun per piece. |

- **Who speaks:** brand material uses "we" (the agency). Social posts and videos where the mascot appears can use Aiphin in the first person ("Hi, I'm Aiphin, your AI teammate").
- **Avoid:** leverage, synergy, holistic, world-class, best-in-class, seamless, revolutionary, game-changer, and fear-based AI messaging ("AI will replace you").
- **CTAs:** WhatsApp first ("Message us on WhatsApp", "Chat with Aiphin"), or the site's lines ("Start a project", "Tell us what's slowing you down"). The low-risk first step to offer is the business diagnostic.
- **Patterns that work:** a contrast line ("We don't sell you a roadmap. We build it and run it."), a concrete before/after ("Leads at 9pm, replies at 9:01pm."), and a specific scene instead of an abstraction ("the quote out before lunch").

### Video voice-over (reels and videos)

- **Aiphin's spoken voice** in every reel is the reference voice the user supplied in October 2026: a 33 s read of the Before/After reel script. It is a warm, expressive male voice with a mid-to-high pitch (about 155 Hz) and a relaxed pace with clear pauses between phrases. If a video needs it and the reference audio isn't in the conversation, ask the user to attach it.
- **Clone it rather than using a stock voice.** Use zero-shot ZipVoice (sherpa-onnx, Apache-2.0). The prompt is a clean 5–6 s clip of the reference plus its exact transcript; the clip "Orders on paper. Stock in one sheet. Sales in another." (3.34–8.80 s) works well. Generate one line per take at speed about 1.1.
- **Check every take.** Run it through a speaker-embedding model (aim for at least 0.8 similarity to the reference) and through speech-to-text. Regenerate any take that clips a word or ends with a question lift on a statement.
- **Always say the name AY-fin.** The reference itself says "EYE-fin", so don't copy that. Spelling it "Aiphin" inside a full sentence produces AY-fin.
- **Pace:** this voice is about 25% slower than a typical TTS read. Plan for roughly 2.5 words per second including pauses, and time the animation to the voice, not the other way round.
- **Fallback:** if cloning isn't possible, the closest stock voice is Kokoro `am_fenrir`.

### Arabic and MENA
