# Style Pack: Minimal Paper

Premium educational look: paper textures, painted illustrations, a thin blue line, serif accents.

## Tokens
- Fonts: Inter (main), Playfair Display Italic (accent). Arabic: see arabic-rtl.md.
- Palette (proposed; confirm from reference videos): paper white [TBD-measure hex], ink black, deep blue accent [TBD-measure hex], plus a mid-gray for secondary text. Keep 60/30/10.
- Textures: paper grain, rough paper (quote scenes), rough cloth (character contrast), paint noise on illustrations, vignette on B-roll.
- Shape language: organic/rough edges on illustrations, thin clean lines for UI and graphs.
- Animation feel: smooth, breathing, camera-driven (slides and tracks), not bouncy. [TBD-measure] scene length 1.5-4 s, ease-in-out.

## Scenes used
All 10 in scene-library.md. Signature scenes: painting + text, line + text, center illustration, quote.

## Known pitfalls
- Heavy texture overlays that do not move with the animation.
- Cuts between two animated scenes with no breathing frame.
- Animation leaving the screen too early (spiral scenes need to stay longer).
- Missing quotation marks on the quote scene.
